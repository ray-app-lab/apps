CHAPTER 1

Free Tier Stocktake Overview

Stocktaking is the process of physically counting goods and recording the quantities. The free tier lets you try out the basic stocktake workflow.

The stocktake flow is: Scan a product → Enter quantity → Save → Review history.

💡 What Is Stocktaking? Stocktaking records the quantity you actually counted in person (the physical count). It is a separate operation from Inventory Management's receiving, shipping, and transfer functions — do not confuse the two.

What You Can Do in the Free Tier

  • Enter or scan a product code / barcode
  • Enter a product name
  • Enter the physical count quantity
  • Enter a memo / note
  • Review stocktake history
  • Save and export within the free tier limit

CHAPTER 3

Step-by-Step Stocktake Instructions

Basic Stocktake Workflow

  1. Tap Stocktake on the top screen.
  2. Scan a barcode, or manually enter the product code.
  3. The product name appears. If the code is unregistered, enter the name manually.
  4. Confirm or select a location (warehouse, store, etc.).
  5. Enter the physically counted quantity.
  6. Enter an optional memo if needed.
  7. Tap Save to record the entry in stocktake history.
  8. Confirm the saved entry in stocktake history.
🚨 Important Always verify the quantity before saving. Entry errors may lead to discrepancies with inventory figures. If you need to correct a saved entry, follow the guidance shown in the app or enter the item again as needed.

Counting Multiple Products in Sequence

  1. After saving the first item, scan the next product's barcode (or enter the code).
  2. Repeat steps 2–7.
  3. Once all products have been entered, review the full stocktake history.
💡 Tip You don't need to complete the stocktake in one session. Even if you close the app mid-count, all saved history is retained.
CHAPTER 4

Barcode, Product Name & Quantity Entry

Barcode Scanning

Launch the camera and hold it over a barcode to scan automatically. If scanning fails, move to a well-lit area or switch to manual entry.

Manual Product Code Entry

If a barcode is unavailable, type the product code directly into the code field. For registered codes, the product name fills in automatically.

Product Name Entry

For unregistered codes, enter the product name manually. The entered name is recorded in stocktake history. Be mindful of record limits when operating without master registration.

Physical Count Quantity Entry

Enter the quantity you physically counted as a numeric value.

⚠ Note Enter the exact quantity you counted. Entry errors cause inventory discrepancies. Always confirm the number before saving.

Memo Entry

You can freely enter work notes or remarks. They are recorded alongside the stocktake history entry.

CHAPTER 5

Handling Locations

The free tier uses a simplified location setup. The default locations are "Warehouse" and "Store."

Locations by Simple Mode

Stocktake ModeAvailable LocationsExample Use
Simple AWarehouse onlyManaging a single warehouse
Simple BWarehouse + StoreStocktaking both warehouse and store
Simple CStore onlyManaging a single store

Select the mode under Settings → Stocktake Settings. Choosing the right mode before you start streamlines the stocktake workflow.

💡 Tip Detailed location management (e.g., shelf numbers) is a Full Unlock feature. Enable it by switching to Detail mode in settings.
⚠ Note Even if detailed locations are registered, they won't appear if the mode is still set to Simple A/B/C. You need to switch to Detail mode.
CHAPTER 6

Reviewing Stocktake History

All saved stocktake entries can be reviewed in Stocktake History.

What You Can See in History

  • Date and time saved
  • Product code and name
  • Physical count quantity entered
  • Location
  • Memo

Free Tier History Limit

The free tier stores up to 30 stocktake history records. If you reach the limit, follow the on-screen guidance. For ongoing use, consider upgrading to Simple Unlock (up to 1,000 records) or Full Unlock (unlimited).

⚠ Note The free tier caps history at 30 records. Exceeding the limit may prevent new stocktake entries from being saved.

CHAPTER 9

Recommended Workflow for Free Tier Users

Best for Small-Scale Operations

If you have 30 or fewer products, the free tier is sufficient for ongoing basic stocktaking.

Export CSVs Regularly to Build a Record

Export to CSV before you approach the 30-record history limit. This accumulates your records in PC or spreadsheet format.

Set Your Simple Mode (A/B/C) in Advance

Choose the right mode (Simple A/B/C) under Settings → Stocktake Settings before starting. This makes each stocktake session smoother.

Plan to Upgrade When Approaching the Limit

As your master and history records approach 30, consider upgrading to Simple Unlock. Hitting the limit may prevent saving or exporting.

💡 Tip Practice with the sample data (create it from Settings) before registering your real products.

📌 Chapter Highlights
