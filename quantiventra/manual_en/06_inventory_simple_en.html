CHAPTER 3

Receiving (Stock In)

The Receive operation increases stock. Use it when goods arrive — for purchasing, intake, and similar transactions.

Receiving Procedure

  1. Go to Inventory Management → Receive.
  2. Scan a barcode or enter the product code.
  3. Confirm the product name. If the code is unregistered, proceed to create a new Product Master entry.
  4. In the new Product Master entry, register the product name, selling price, and reorder point.
  5. After saving, return to the Receive screen — the registered product code and name are auto-populated.
  6. Select the destination location (warehouse, store, etc.).
  7. Enter the quantity received.
  8. Tap Save. The quantity is added to stock immediately.
  9. Confirm the update in Stock Check or Inventory History.
💡 About Product Master Attributes Selling price and reorder point are properties managed in the Product Master — they are not entered per receiving transaction. In the normal Receive screen, focus on entering quantity, location, and memo.
⚠ Note Receiving is reflected in stock immediately. Always confirm the quantity and location before saving. If you notice an incorrect quantity after saving, review Inventory History and follow the in-app guidance to correct it, reverse it, or record an adjustment as appropriate.
CHAPTER 4

Shipping (Stock Out)

The Ship operation decreases stock. Use it for sales, outbound transfers, deliveries, and similar transactions.

Shipping Procedure

  1. Go to Inventory Management → Ship.
  2. Scan a barcode or enter the product code.
  3. Confirm the product name.
  4. Select the source location (where the stock is leaving from).
  5. Enter the shipping quantity.
  6. Tap Save. The quantity is subtracted from stock immediately.
  7. Confirm the update in Stock Check or Inventory History.
💡 About the Stock Field The stock field on the Ship screen shows the current stock for the selected product and location — it is a display field, not an editable input. Stock decreases as a result of saving the shipping quantity.
🚨 Important Do not enter a shipping quantity that exceeds current stock. Check the stock level by location before entering the quantity to ship.
CHAPTER 5

Stock Check

Stock Check initially displays the combined stock total per product. The replenishment check compares the total stock across all "Include in replenishment check" ON locations against the reorder point in the Product Master.

Stock Check Procedure

  1. Go to Inventory Management → Stock Check.
  2. Find the product you want to check (search or scroll).
  3. Identify products below their reorder point (shown as needing replenishment).
  4. Tap a product to view the location-by-location breakdown, history, and detailed information. In the location breakdown view, the replenishment check is not shown or is treated as inactive.
CHAPTER 6

Inventory History

Inventory History shows the log of all receiving, shipping, transfer, stocktake adjustment, initial stock, and CSV import operations.

What You Can See in Inventory History

  • Date/time and operation type (receive, ship, transfer, adjustment, etc.)
  • Product code and name
  • Location and quantity change
  • Memo
💡 Tip If your stock counts don't add up, scroll back through inventory history to check for entry errors or unexpected adjustments.

Stock Transfers

A stock transfer moves inventory quantities from one location to another.

⚠ Note Do not transfer between the same location. If you only have one location (e.g., Simple A), stock transfers are unnecessary — use receiving, shipping, or a correction instead.
💡 Tip To use detailed locations (e.g., shelf numbers), you need to switch to Detail mode in settings. Detail mode is used when accessing Full Unlock features.

CSV Import / Export

Inventory Management lets you import, export, and back up stock data using CSV files.

🚨 Important CSV import operations can significantly alter your data. Always export a backup CSV before importing.

Inventory CSV Specifications

CSV TypePurposeKey ColumnsRequired/OptionalNotesAvailable In
Stock CSV (with location column) Import location-specific stock data location, item_code, quantity, product_name location, item_code, quantity required; product_name conditionally required product_name column required for unregistered codes Inventory Simple Unlock or above
Stock CSV (without location column) Import stock data for a specified location item_code, quantity, product_name item_code, quantity required Select the target location at import time Inventory Simple Unlock or above
Backup CSV Full data backup and restore Products, current stock, locations, categories — multiple files Full backup set required May be split across multiple files Inventory Simple Unlock or above

Using the Column Mapping Screen

When importing a CSV, you select which column maps to product code, product name, quantity, location, etc.

  1. Select the CSV file.
  2. On the column mapping screen, assign each CSV column to its corresponding field (product code, quantity, etc.).
  3. Confirm the assignments and run the import.
⚠ Common CSV Import Errors
  • Missing required columns
  • The same column assigned to more than one field
  • Quantity column is empty or non-numeric
  • No product name column selected for unregistered codes
  • No location column and no target location selected
  • CSV is empty or contains no importable rows
CHAPTER 9

Replace Current Stock

Replace Current Stock is a CSV import mode that treats the file's quantities as the new current stock. Existing history is preserved, and the difference is recorded as an adjustment history entry.

🚨 Important: Use Replace Current Stock with Caution
  • Stock rows not present in the CSV may be set to 0.
  • This operation can significantly impact existing stock data — always take a backup first.
  • Read the confirmation screen carefully before executing.

Replace Current Stock Procedure

  1. Export a backup CSV (required).
  2. Go to Inventory Management → CSV Import → Replace Current Stock.
  3. Select the CSV file to import.
  4. Confirm column mapping on the mapping screen.
  5. On the confirmation screen, carefully review the replacement details (adjustment content).
  6. If everything looks correct, execute the import.
  7. Verify the adjustment entries in inventory history.
CHAPTER 10

Add as Receiving

Add as Receiving is a CSV import mode that adds the file's quantities to existing stock as a receiving transaction — unlike Replace Current Stock, it adds to, rather than overwriting, current stock.

Import ModeBehaviorExample Use
Replace Current StockTreats CSV quantities as the new current stock. Records differences as adjustment historyPost-stocktake stock correction, initial setup
Add as ReceivingAdds CSV quantities to current stock as receivingBulk receiving import
💡 Tip Use Replace Current Stock when setting up inventory for the first time, and Add as Receiving when adding to existing stock. Confirm which you need before running the import.
CHAPTER 11

Important Notes

⚠ Setting Up for Detailed Locations To use detailed locations, switch to Detail mode in settings. Detailed locations will not appear if you are still on Simple A/B/C.
⚠ Verify Location and Quantity Before Acting Receiving, shipping, transfers, and CSV imports are reflected in stock immediately. Confirm the location and quantity before each operation.
🚨 Back Up Before CSV Operations Always take a backup before Replace Current Stock, Add as Receiving, and backup restore operations.
CHAPTER 12

Recommended Workflow

Record Receiving and Shipping Daily

  • Register incoming stock via Receive each time goods arrive.
  • Record outgoing stock via Ship each time goods leave.
  • Set reorder points in the Product Master and use Stock Check to monitor replenishment timing.

Regular Stock Checks

  • Check stock counts regularly — weekly, monthly, or as needed.
  • Combine with stocktaking to periodically verify that physical counts match your inventory records.

Regular Backups

  • Export a backup CSV at least once a month and save it to your PC or cloud storage.
💡 Consider Upgrading to Full Unlock If You…
  • Need advanced code/location rules or extended CSV settings
  • Need to directly edit the registered cumulative stock total
  • Have more than 300 products

📌 Chapter Highlights

  • Simple Unlock raises limits: up to 300 master records, 1,000 history records, 300 export records.
  • Receiving and shipping are reflected in stock immediately — confirm quantity and location before saving.
  • Inventory History shows a complete log of all receiving, shipping, transfer, and adjustment operations.
  • CSV import has two modes — Replace Current Stock and Add as Receiving. Always back up before either.
  • Locations are set via Simple A/B/C. Switch to Detail mode for detailed locations.
  • Directly editing the registered cumulative total requires Full Unlock.

🔍 Notes Before Use

  • Use Location Master within the options shown in the app. For detailed location management, check the current mode and consider Full Unlock features when needed.
  • If a receiving or shipping entry was saved incorrectly, review Inventory History and follow the in-app guidance to correct it, reverse it, or record an adjustment as appropriate.
  • When exporting a backup CSV set, keep the generated files together and store them safely.
  • On the stock transfer screen, confirm both the source location and destination location before saving the transfer.
  • For CSV import, review the column mapping and final replacement confirmation screen before running the operation.
