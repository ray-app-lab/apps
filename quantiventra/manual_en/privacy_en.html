Privacy Policy

Ray App Lab ("the Developer") establishes this Privacy Policy to explain how user information is handled in the Android application "Quantiventra" ("the App").

1. Collection and Use of Personal Information

During normal stocktake and inventory operations, the App does not send stocktake, inventory-management, or other business data entered in the App to a server operated by the Developer for collection. Names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and similar personal information are not requested as part of the App's normal stocktake and inventory features.

2. Purpose of Device Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions solely to enable its core features. No permission is used beyond its stated purpose.

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used exclusively for scanning barcodes and 2D codes. No photos or videos are recorded, saved, or transmitted.
  • File selection & sharing: CSV files are read (imported) or written (exported) only to files or destinations selected by the user. The App does not request persistent storage permission for unrestricted access to device files.

3. Data Storage and Security

During normal stocktake and inventory operations, data entered or scanned in the App is stored on the user's device. The Developer does not operate an external server to collect that business data. Users are responsible for managing and backing up their own data.

When purchasing a plan, restoring a previous purchase, opening the Google Play Store page, or opening external links, network communication may occur through Google Play, a browser, or the relevant external service. Please refer to Google Play or the relevant service for purchase terms, refund terms, and how information is handled by that service.

4. Changes to This Policy

The Developer may revise this Policy in response to changes in law or app updates. Any significant changes will be announced on this page.

5. Contact

For inquiries about this Policy, please use the contact information on the official website or the developer contact listed on the app store page.
