Privacy Policy

How the Focus Flame iOS app handles information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Focus Flame iOS app (bundle identifier com.rayapplab.focusflame). It describes the current iOS version of the app. If a different platform or a future version uses different features or third-party services, its data practices may differ and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

2. No account or developer-operated cloud service

Focus Flame does not require an account. We do not operate a developer server for collecting, storing, or synchronizing your focus history, settings, camera input, or other in-app data. The iOS app does not use CloudKit synchronization.

3. Information stored on your device

To provide the app's timer, program, and insights features, Focus Flame stores the following information in its local app storage:

CategoryExamples and purpose
Focus history and session-recovery stateCompleted session identifier, selected mode, completed focus duration, and completion date and time. While a focus session is running or a completed session is being safely recorded, the app also keeps the minimal session identifier, mode, and timing state needed to handle an interruption or safely finish the record. These records support your history, Focus Points, calendar, and program progress.
PreferencesTimer, goal, theme, sound, language, notification, and accessibility-related choices. These let the app remember your selected experience.
Premium statusThe current Premium entitlement state and its verification time. This lets the app reflect Apple purchase entitlement locally.

Depending on your device and account settings, iOS may include app data in device backups. Focus Flame does not control those backup settings and does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

4. Camera

Camera use is optional. If you turn on focus sensing for an eligible focus session and allow camera access, the app uses the front camera during that session to estimate movement on-device. Camera frames are used transiently for that estimate. Focus Flame does not save, display, upload, or transmit camera images or video.

You can change camera permission at any time in iOS Settings. Focus Flame does not use the camera for exercise sessions.

5. Local notifications

If you grant permission and enable a reminder, Focus Flame schedules a daily local notification on your device. It does not use push notifications, collect a device token, or operate a notification server.

6. Purchases

Focus Flame may offer Premium subscriptions through Apple's StoreKit when products are configured and made available. Apple handles payment processing and the associated Apple account information. Focus Flame does not receive or store your payment card number or Apple account password.

The app checks Apple purchase entitlement and retains only the local Premium status and verification time needed to reflect access in the app. Apple processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

7. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

The current iOS version does not display advertising and does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or tracking SDK. Focus Flame does not sell personal information and does not share your locally stored focus data with a developer-operated server.

If advertising, analytics, a cloud feature, or another third-party service is added in a future release, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before enabling that feature.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Your saved app data remains in local app storage until it is removed through the device or app lifecycle. You can stop using camera access or notifications through iOS Settings. You can remove the app and its local app container by deleting Focus Flame from your device; device backups, if any, are managed through your own device and account settings.

Because Focus Flame does not provide developer-hosted accounts or a developer-operated cloud database for this app, we do not hold a separate server copy of your focus history to delete or export.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, its data practices, legal requirements, or platform requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

For a privacy question, email ray.app.lab@gmail.com.

Important: This page describes the current iOS implementation. The App Store privacy declaration and any future third-party service configuration must remain consistent with this policy.
