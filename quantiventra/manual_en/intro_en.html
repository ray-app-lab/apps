Quantiventra

Keep routine stocktaking and inventory work moving on the device, even in warehouses, stores, or other sites with unstable connectivity.

Some functions, such as purchase verification, purchase restoration, opening the store page, or opening external links, may require an internet connection.

Stocktaking and inventory management, streamlined for everyday field work.

Scan barcodes and QR codes, enter quantities, manage locations, and import or export CSV data in a workflow suited to warehouses, stores, and other work sites.

Key Features

The sections below describe the main features Quantiventra provides — from stocktaking and receiving to shipping and stock checking — and how they support day-to-day work on site.

📸 Camera Scanning

Use your device camera to read barcodes and 2D codes such as QR codes.

🔍 Product Name Search (Partial Match)

If no barcode is available, type part of a product name to quickly filter results with partial-match (contains) search.

📍 Location Management

Record and manage storage locations such as shelf numbers and areas. In stock checking, you can choose which locations count toward replenishment calculations.

🧮 Smart Calculations & Built-in Calculator

Enter expressions directly into quantity fields (e.g., 10*5+3) and use the built-in calculator to make on-site tallying easier.

📄 Seamless CSV Integration

Import and export master data, stocktake data, and inventory data as CSV files in a format that is easy to review on a PC and use for backups.

🔒 Offline & Privacy-First

Routine stocktake and inventory work is handled mainly on the device, helping work continue even when connectivity is unstable. Network communication may be required for Google Play purchase verification, purchase restoration, store navigation, or external links.

Plans & Feature Unlocks

Quantiventra starts with a free tier, with optional upgrades to unlock only the features you need. Stocktake and Inventory Management each have Free, Simple, and Full levels, and All Languages Unlock is handled as a separate add-on. If you use both Stocktake and Inventory Management, a bundle purchase may be better value than buying each separately. Check Google Play for current pricing and purchase terms.

  • Stocktake: Choose from Free, Simple, or Full to match your workflow.
  • Inventory Management: Daily operations such as receiving, shipping, and stock checking are available, while capacity and advanced settings vary by unlock level.
  • All Languages Unlock: A separate add-on to enable all supported display languages.

Detailed instructions for the free tier and each unlock level are available in the English manual.
