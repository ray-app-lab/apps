CHAPTER 1

What Is Quantiventra?

1-1. About the App

Quantiventra is a portmanteau of "Quantity" and "Inventory." It is an Android app that streamlines stocktaking and inventory management by using your smartphone camera to scan barcodes.

Simply point the camera at a barcode to identify a product automatically, then enter and save the quantity — an intuitive workflow that takes your paper-based stocktake digital. Results are saved as CSV files, making it easy to work with Excel or Google Sheets.

1-2. Key Features

📦 Stock Counting

Quickly identify products by barcode scan and record quantities instantly. The built-in calculator makes tallying multiple locations simple.

🗂️ Master Data Management

Register products, categories, and locations as master data. Bulk import via CSV is also supported.

📍 Location Management

Register multiple locations such as warehouses, stores, and shelves, and manage stock by location. You can also configure which locations count toward replenishment calculations.

📊 CSV Import / Export

Export stock lists, backups, and history as CSV. Easy to aggregate in Excel or Google Sheets.

🌐 Multilingual Support

Supports 12 languages including English, Japanese, Chinese, and Korean. Automatically matches your device language.

⭐ Full Unlock Features

Removes capacity limits and enables detailed rules, extended CSV settings, Location Master management, and other advanced configuration options. P

1-3. How Camera Scanning Works

When a barcode falls within the guide frame (a blue or yellow outline) displayed in the center of the screen, the app auto-recognizes it after detecting the same code in 3 consecutive frames. This triple-confirmation approach prevents misreads while ensuring reliable detection.

💡 If a code isn't being recognized The barcode must fit entirely inside the guide frame. Codes outside the frame will not be detected. Adjust the camera position until the code is fully contained within the frame.

The app has two main modules — Stocktake and Inventory Management — with master data management, settings, and CSV import/export as shared features.

CHAPTER 3

Installation & First Launch

3-1. Installation

Search for "Quantiventra" on the Google Play Store, or scan the QR code at the top of this page to go directly to the store listing.

  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Search for "Quantiventra" or scan the QR code to open the store page.
  3. Tap Install.

3-2. First Launch Flow

  1. Tap the app icon to launch.
  2. A camera permission dialog appears — tap Allow.
  3. If a sample data prompt appears, choose Create or Skip.
  4. The app's data folder is automatically created in internal storage.
  5. Once the top screen appears, initial setup is complete.
⚠ If You Deny Camera Permission Barcode scanning will be unavailable. To grant it later: Device Settings → Apps → Quantiventra → Permissions → Camera → Enable.

3-3. Transferring to a New Device

  1. On your old device, export or share your data via Settings → Export or Settings → Share.
  2. Sign in to the new device with the same Google account.
  3. Install Quantiventra from Google Play.
  4. Tap Settings → Restore Purchases to restore purchased plans.
  5. Import your backed-up master CSV via Settings → Import Master CSV.
🚨 Warning: Uninstalling the App Uninstalling the app deletes all data in internal storage. Back up important data regularly using Export or Share.

Opening a CSV in Excel

CSV files are saved in UTF-8. The following steps are recommended for opening them in Excel.

  1. Open Excel and go to the Data tab → From Text/CSV.
  2. Select your CSV file.
  3. Choose Unicode (UTF-8) as the encoding.
  4. Click Load.
⚠ Don't Double-Click the CSV File Directly Opening a CSV by double-clicking may result in garbled characters. Always use the Data tab import method.

Recommended Backup Procedure

  1. Tap Settings → Export (Select Folder).
  2. Create and select a backup folder in your device's Documents folder — e.g., inventory_backup_20250401.
  3. Done once the history and master CSVs are saved.
  4. For extra safety, copy to a PC via Google Drive or USB cable.

CHAPTER 6

Getting Started (Beginner's Guide)

Step 1: Try It with Sample Data

  1. Launch the app; if prompted to create sample data, choose Create.
  2. Open Inventory Management from the top screen and check sample stock levels.
  3. Open Stocktake and try barcode scanning and quantity entry.
  4. Try exporting a CSV.
  5. Delete sample data before going live (from the Settings screen).

Step 2: Register Your Product Master

  1. Open Master Management from the top screen.
  2. Go to Product Master, create a new entry, and enter a product code and name.
  3. Set a category, price, and reorder point as needed.
  4. Register warehouses, stores, etc. in the Location Master when your plan and in-app screen allow it.

Step 3: Start Daily Operations

  1. When receiving stock: go to Inventory Management → Receive, scan the barcode, and enter the quantity.
  2. When shipping: go to Inventory Management → Ship and follow the same process.
  3. For stocktaking: record physical counts from Stocktake.
💡 Tip Practice with sample data first until you're comfortable, then register your actual products.

CHAPTER 12

Troubleshooting

Barcode not scanning / camera not responding
  • Check that the camera switch (top-right) is turned ON.
  • Keep the barcode fully inside the guide frame — codes outside the frame will not be detected.
  • In Product Barcode Scan Settings, verify that the enabled formats match the product barcodes you actually use.
  • If the barcode is torn or dirty, use manual entry instead.
  • Verify camera permission: Settings → Apps → Quantiventra → Permissions → Camera.
Product names not showing after importing master CSV
  • Confirm the CSV is encoded in UTF-8 (Shift-JIS will cause garbled text).
  • Verify the header row matches the required format: barcode,name,theoretical_stock,actual_stock_total.
  • If a product name contains a comma, enclose it in double quotes: e.g., "Bolt, M6".
  • Re-run Import Master CSV.
Cannot save / "Save failed" message appears
  • Check that your device has sufficient free storage.
  • You cannot save an entry with no barcode and no product name entered.
  • Try closing and restarting the app.
Share or Export produces no file
  • If there are 0 history records, the file will be empty — save at least one entry first.
  • When exporting, make sure to select a folder in the dialog and tap Select.
  • Confirm the target app (e.g., email) is installed.
Stock quantities don't match
  • Review inventory history for receiving, shipping, transfer, and stocktake adjustment records.
  • If you ran a CSV stock replace, items not in the CSV may have been set to 0.
  • If stocktake apply was run, discrepancies are recorded as "Stocktake Adjustment."
  • Cross-reference with your backup CSV.
Locations not showing / can't find a location
  • Check whether the stocktake mode is still set to Simple A/B/C. Switch to Detail mode to use detailed locations.
  • Check that the location is not set to Inactive in the Location Master.
Purchased plan features not available
  • Tap Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Verify you are signed in with the Google account used for the purchase.
  • Try again with a working internet connection (required to verify purchases).
  • Confirm you are using the version installed via the Google Play Store.
Data disappeared after changing devices
  • Data is stored in the app's internal storage and is lost when the app is uninstalled or the device is changed.
  • It is strongly recommended to back up your data regularly via Export or Share.
  • If you have a backup, restore it using Import Master CSV.
Screen keeps rotating, making work difficult
  • If you have Simple Unlock or above: enable Settings → Lock Screen Rotation.
  • If on the free tier: use your device's built-in rotation lock (available from the notification panel).
Language won't change
  • Manually select a language in Settings → Language.
  • Check the selected app language and your device language settings.
  • Unsupported device languages will default to English.

Glossary

TermDefinition
StocktakeThe act of physically counting and recording the quantity of goods in stock
Physical countThe actual quantity counted on the floor
Theoretical stockThe stock quantity calculated by the system
Discrepancy / VarianceThe difference between the physical count and theoretical stock
ProductAn individual item that holds inventory
CategoryA grouping used to classify products
LocationA place where stock is held (e.g., warehouse, store, shelf)
Include in replenishment checkA setting that includes a location's stock in the total used for replenishment decisions
Stock / InventoryThe current quantity on hand
Receiving (Stock In)An operation that increases stock
Shipping (Stock Out)An operation that decreases stock
Stock TransferMoving stock from one location to another when the required products, stock, and locations are available
Inventory HistoryA log of receiving, shipping, transfer, and stocktake adjustment operations
Reorder PointA reference quantity used to judge when stock is running low
Product CodeAn internal code used to identify a product
Registered Cumulative TotalThe accumulated total quantity saved to date
CSVA comma-separated text file that can be opened in spreadsheet applications
BackupData saved for recovery or reference purposes
Replace Current StockA CSV import mode that treats the file's quantities as the new current stock
Add as Stock-InA CSV import mode that adds the file's quantities as receiving
Specified Stocktake Date BasisA method that recalculates stock from a past stocktake date as the baseline
