Locuvo Privacy Policy (iOS)

App: Locuvo (iOS)
Provider: Masahiro Ito (operating as RayAppLab)
Last updated: September 14, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS version of the travel-planning app "Locuvo" (the "App") handles user information. For the Android version, see this page.

In short: the App stores your travel plans, records, photos, and location history only on your device. We do not operate our own servers and do not collect or store your data. The Free plan shows ads served by Google AdMob.

1. Our approach

The App stores your travel plans and records only on your device. We do not operate our own servers, and we do not collect or store your travel data, photos, or location history.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored in the App's private storage on your device, based on your input: travel plans (titles, dates, place names/addresses/coordinates, transport modes and times, notes), diary entries and photos (taken with the in-app camera or picked from your device), GPS trails recorded during trip execution (see 3), app settings, free-plan usage counters, and purchase state. None of this is sent to us. You can delete it with the in-app deletion tools or by uninstalling the App.

3. Location (GPS)

  • Only when you start a trip's execution mode does the App record your location, to draw your trail, show your position, and detect arrivals. To keep recording while you travel, the App may ask for the "Always Allow" location permission.
  • Recorded locations are stored only on your device and are never transmitted externally.
  • Location permission is optional; planning, viewing, and diary features work without it.

4. Camera and photos

The camera is used to attach photos to your diary and calendar. Photos are stored on your device. With your permission, photos you take in the App (trip covers, diary entries, calendar days) and GPS trail map images are saved to the iOS Photos app. Nothing is transmitted externally.

5. Notifications

Notifications are used for departure reminders.

6. Maps, routes (Apple services), and other external services

Map display and travel-time calculation use Apple's built-in map service (MapKit). The displayed map area and the origin/destination coordinates for route calculation are sent to Apple, and are handled under the Apple Privacy Policy.

On the Free plan, the map for looking up coordinates loads map images from the OpenStreetMap tile servers. The displayed map area and your IP address are sent to OpenStreetMap, and are handled under the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

When you tap a navigation button, the App hands the destination coordinates to the map app you choose (Apple Maps or Google Maps) and opens it. From that point, the chosen app's policy applies.

7. Purchases (In-App Purchase)

Paid plans and tickets are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your card details. To reflect your purchase state, the App receives purchase information from Apple (purchased products, expiry, etc.) and stores it on your device.

8. Advertising

The Free plan shows ads via Google AdMob. To serve ads, information such as your IP address and device information is sent to Google and handled under the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law, a consent prompt (Google UMP) is shown before ads are served, and you can change your choices anytime in Settings → Privacy settings (ad consent). Ads are shown only while your base plan is Free: they do not appear on the Premium or Platinum base plans or while a Light Ticket base right is active. An add-on subscription that does not change the base plan (Fandom Journal) remains subject to ads. We use no analytics or third-party measurement SDKs.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13.

10. Backup and deletion

  • Backups are exported as a file (JSON) only when you choose to, and you control where they are saved.
  • All data lives on your device; deleting it in the App or uninstalling the App removes it. Since we hold no user data on servers, no server-side deletion request is needed.

11. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced on this page and, where appropriate, in the App.
